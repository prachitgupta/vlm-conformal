Title: Clear Corridor Cruise

Task:

- Current position NED is (0.00, 0.00, -2.50) m.
- Current velocity NED is (0.00, 0.00, 0.00) m/s.
- Goal position NED is (8.00, 0.50, -2.50) m.

- south_crate: axis-aligned box centered at NED (5.60, -2.80) m with north/east footprint 1.20 x 1.20 m.

Hard constraints:
- Maintain at least 0.80 m clearance from every listed obstacle.
- The first waypoint must be within 3.00 m of the current position.
- Consecutive waypoint spacing should stay between 0.20 m and 1.40 m.
- Use selected_waypoint_index = 0 unless there is a strong safety reason to choose otherwise.

Expected qualitative behavior:
- The correct behavior is close to a straight line toward the goal.
- A slight east bias is acceptable, but the path should remain local and smooth.

Required deliverable:
- Return exactly five 2D NED waypoints, select the immediate next waypoint, and explain briefly why the trajectory is safe and goal-directed.
